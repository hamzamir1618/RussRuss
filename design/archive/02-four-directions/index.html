RUSSRUSS, four ways

Four art directions for the landing page. All four share the same scroll choreography: a loader, the cup drop, the hero press, a pinned product row, a manifesto with counters, and a juice-fill outro. Each sits on a white canvas with its own look, accent colors and scene transition. Open one, scroll it top to bottom, and use the bar at the bottom to switch.

A · Retro sunshine

Sunburst Stand

A 1970s roadside juice stand at sunset. Spinning sun rays, stacked wave bands, groovy type with layered shadows, and a sun that expands to carry you into the next scene.
