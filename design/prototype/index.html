02 · Voice

Say what happens

We sound like the person behind the counter: plain, specific and a little dry. The type is loud enough already, so the words don't need to be.

Facts over adjectivesTimes, weights, ingredients and steps. "Pressed at 6 am" says more than "fresh".
Short, and in "we"Most sentences under twelve words. We do the work, so we say "we".
One joke per sectionA light aside is fine ("The ginger shows up a second later"). Two is a bit.
No health claimsNo detox, cleanse benefits, superfoods or anything a label can't back up.

04 · How it's made

Four steps

The manifesto became a walkthrough: pick, cut, press, pour. Each step photo sits in a circular frame, so the press, cut off at the edges in its original photo, now reads as a clean round window.

Frame anatomy
  1. Text ringRighteous caps on a circle path: "STEP 03 • PRESS •". Turns every 40s.
  2. RingsA 5px pink or tangerine ring at 80% diameter and a dotted cocoa ring at 97%.
  3. WindowA circle clip at 79% diameter over a sunflower-to-tangerine gradient with slow rays.
  4. Photo fitPick: four fruits composed inside. Cut: contained, spinning. Press: cropped to the spout, so the photo's own edges disappear. Pour: contained at 94% height.
  5. Around itA rainbow arc below, daisies, sparkles, a sun icon, halftone dots and a squiggle, all in motion.

StackGSAP 3.12 + ScrollTrigger + Lenis, about 40 KB gzipped. All motion lives in one engine.js with a small config per page.
FontsShrikhand, Monoton, Righteous and Rubik, all free on Google Fonts. Self-host the four files in production.
Modern CSSThe rolling baseline needs CSS sin() and @property. Both are supported in current Chrome, Safari and Firefox; older browsers get a straight line.
Assets20 photos became 35 transparent WebP cutouts, 2.7 MB in total. All decoration is inline SVG, so it adds nothing to the download.
Reduced motionNo loader, no pins, no particles, and stickers stand still. Every section renders as a static page.
