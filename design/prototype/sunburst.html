Step 1 · The morning delivery

How a cup gets made

  1. Pick
  2. Cut
  3. Press
  4. Pour

Step 01 · Pick

5:00a.m., when the fruit shows up

We buy what's ripe that morning.

So the menu moves with the seasons. In winter, that means a lot of citrus.

Step 02 · Cut

3.2pounds of fruit in every cup

Everything is washed and cut by hand.

Peels off, seeds out. Nothing goes in to bulk it up.

Step 03 · Press

4tons of slow pressure

Squeezed, not spun.

A hydraulic press pushes the juice out of the pulp, so nothing heats up on the way.

Step 04 · Pour

72hours before it fades

Into cups within the hour.

We don't pasteurize, so it doesn't keep forever. Drink it within three days.
